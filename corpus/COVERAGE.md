# Schema-cell coverage

Generated by `tools/cell_coverage.py` from `schema/schema.json`, the manifest and `corpus/cell-classes.json`. Do not edit by hand.

The corpus covers 440 of the 538 cells a ledger reader can meet (81.8%); of the 465 of those that can occur in a closed ledger, 94.6%.

## What a cell is

A cell is a union arm, or a member of an enum that appears as a value in its own right. A member of an enum used only as a union's discriminant is not counted again: it is covered exactly when its arm is. The schema has 73 such enums, which is why it has 659 cells, not 1188.

Coverage is measured over the cells a ledger reader can meet: those in the 358 of 477 types that `LedgerCloseMeta` reaches. 121 cells are in types no ledger carries (listed at the end).
