# Mutation testing of the replay oracle

Generated by `mutants.py` in this directory; do not edit by hand. A toy indexer that stores what the oracle checks, in wallet-backend's vocabulary, is changed one bug at a time, and the harness's own verdict judges each mutant on 16 corpus ledgers (the baseline passes on 16). The method is in the script's docstring.

**21 of the 21 mutants the ledgers exercise are killed.** Survivors: none. The checks require every row the ledger implies, and a state-change row stored more than once fails; other extra rows are caught only by the participant rule and by the balance cross-check on accounts, so a contract's balance is caught only by chance. Where a mutant fails on only some of the ledgers it changes, the others happen to satisfy every check.
